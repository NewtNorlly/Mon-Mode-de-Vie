# Mon Mode de Vie

Mon Mode de Vie is a personal digital living space. Visitors meet the author through conversation, dated stories, schedules, photographs and deliberately chosen side paths rather than through a conventional résumé page.

## Language

**MMV**:
The canonical abbreviation of Mon Mode de Vie.
_Avoid_: MMW

**Paper Salon / 纸上会客厅**:
The selected visual and interaction direction for the first Home screen: an editorial paper surface inside a stable three-column workspace.
_Avoid_: Landing page, dashboard

**Home Guide / Home 引导**:
The authored branching conversation that welcomes a visitor and lets them choose what to discover.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, AI chat

**Anniversary Story / 纪念日故事**:
A date-matched dialogue adaptation linked to a real Journal entry.
_Avoid_: Daily prompt, generated story

**Now**:
The public, privacy-filtered view of the author's calendar and current rhythm of life.
_Avoid_: Live location tracker

**Journal**:
The dated archive of the author's writing, including source text and dialogue adaptations where available.
_Avoid_: Blog feed

**Album**:
The module that stores and presents the author's photographs.
_Avoid_: Collection, 收藏与兴趣

**Cloud**:
The fifth primary navigation item and expandable utility entrance for theme, language, contact and links.
_Avoid_: Right sidebar toggle, More menu

**Author Picks / 作者精选**:
The left-column area containing exactly one author-selected, click-to-load embedded video for Book, Film and Music. Visible provider/category overlays are intentionally absent.
_Avoid_: Album, recommendations feed

**Small Thoughts / 小巧思**:
The right-column area containing the author's envelope message and the narrow-column Paper Run mini game.
_Avoid_: Cloud, utility menu

**Paper Run / 纸上漫游**:
MMV's four-language, theme-aware endless runner in a sandboxed right-column frame. Its mechanics are adapted from Chromium's BSD-licensed runner reference, while its paper cat, book obstacles and public identity are MMV's own.
_Avoid_: Chrome Dino, Edge game, official Chromium game
