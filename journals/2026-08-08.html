In the evening, my girl cousin was studying like crazy, while my boy cousin, that slacker, was just lying there. Fell asleep studying, snoozed all the way till his parents came home.

Third Uncle and Aunt got back around 8 pm. When they arrived, my girl cousin and I were studying in the first-floor room—we hadn't eaten yet.

Third Uncle was in a great mood tonight. He cooked a whole spread of amazing dishes all by himself, and even scrubbed the kitchen and bathroom clean. The food was rich and hearty—I ate so comfortably! Third Aunt asked about my girl cousin's study progress; Third Uncle didn't ask much about my boy cousin's.

After 10 pm, we watched a blockbuster called "Tiger Hunt 2," all the way past midnight. It got pretty wild!

Third Uncle had asked me for movie recommendations. I threw out: search "Hill 205," it's about the Russo-Japanese War, or "Our Fathers' War," about Germany in WWII. Too bad that Galaxy Kiwi app had no full movies at all—just commentary clips 🤔🤔🤔

At night, Third Uncle slept in the big room. I rushed to shower before my boy cousin could, then went up to the second floor and sprawled out like a starfish on the small bed in the big room.
