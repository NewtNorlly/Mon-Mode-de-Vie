柴江赣北 : Master Xia's writing and photography are both natural and freewheeling, so I'm reposting them here: On the road I met a mountain forest photographer, perhaps Clint Eastwood from “The Bridges of Madison County.” On the rocky gravel path I met a few stunt riders cycling uphill and young students running cross-country. The broken walls and piles of roots in the middle section of the mountain resemble scenes from Bi Gan's films or some art-house movies. At the edge of the mountain, two women traveling together with the white rock area and the pine needle forest looked as if they had stepped into an oil painting — truly like a dream. What a Yujiashan!
