Rewind the clock 36 hours. He had naively believed that when an AI wrote a post, roughly 70% of the output was processed basic facts, and the remaining 30% was probably fabricated out of thin air. And as a human, he only needed to write 30% of the post's content himself — how easy that would be.

But reality was not like that at all. When an AI wrote a post, only 30% of the output was basic facts, another 40% was reworked from other materials, and the remaining 30% was entirely made up by the AI!

This matter has become a cautionary lesson; next time he would never dare ask an AI to write a Party-and-government post again!

Cette affaire est devenue une leçon ; la prochaine fois, il n'oserait plus jamais demander à une IA de rédiger un post du Parti et du gouvernement !
