湖北省武汉市

浔阳江头，

列车北上，

蓦然回首，

我才意识到：

回浔阳看牙医，

又累又无聊！

关键还占用时间！

趁现在还很无聊，

就清理一下没用的截屏

我记得，有个B站UP主叫“魏都人”，估计是摄影师或剪辑师。点开主页看一眼，你会发现，他把哔哩当网盘了！

深有同感啊，手机系统相册的好多照片，很有时代价值，但都成故纸堆了。干脆把它们丢到公共网络空间里，再从手机中删掉！

By the Xunyang River,

the train heads north,

I suddenly turn back to look,

and I realize:

going back to Xunyang to see the dentist,

is tiring and boring!

And it eats up my time!

While I'm still bored,

I'll clear out some useless screenshots

I remember there's a Bilibili creator called “魏都人”, probably a photographer or video editor. Open their profile and you'll see that they've been using Bilibili as their cloud drive!

I feel exactly the same. Lots of photos in my phone's system album have real historical value, but they've all become a pile of old papers. Might as well toss them into a public online space, then delete them from my phone!

je me retourne soudain,

et je me rends compte :

revenir à Xunyang voir le dentiste,

c'est fatiguant et ennuyeux !
