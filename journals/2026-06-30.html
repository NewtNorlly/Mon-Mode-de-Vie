🌤☕ Afternoon to Evening · Major Surgery on the Hanako Skill Library

Today I did the boldest thing since installing Hanako: completely reorganized 53 scattered skill folders into 5 Bee categories.

The trigger was simple — I asked Hanako to copy three web search skill files to the desktop, and discovered that web-search-bing-cn, multi-search-engine, and deep-research, three heavily overlapping search skills, were scattered across three different folders. And then it spiraled out of control.

From 17:43 straight through to 21:30 — four hours in total, four rounds of restructuring.

Round 1: 53 → 38 Merged 5 groups of overlapping skills (academic ×9, web-search ×3, media ×4, study ×9, design ×3), and renamed 5 skills whose names weren't intuitive enough (fable5 → deep-refactor, karpathy-guidelines → karpathy-code-guide, zoom-out → big-picture, etc.).

Round 2: 38 → 25 Continued absorbing: grill-bee (interrogation dialogues), skill-bee (skill management), outlook-bee (calendar), plan-bee (requirement documentation), refactor-bee (code refactoring), qa-bee (diagnostic triage), meta-bee (metacognitive mode switching). The deprecated github was simply deleted.

Round 3: 25 → 16 Classified and absorbed all remaining standalone skills: dev-bee, git-bee, and docs-bee were newly created; handoff → meta-bee; weather → outlook-bee; user-guide → skill-bee. git-guardrails was deprecated and deleted outright.

At this point Hanako raised a key question: “Each small skill must also be highly cohesive internally, and loosely coupled with the others.” Coincidentally, I was looking at the open-source project awesome-ai-research-writing (18 practical Prompt templates + Agent Skills recommendations), and decided to fold it into academic-bee as a reference library.

Then came another round of reform: I moved the search trio of the “search literature → write paper” pipeline (bing-cn, multi-engine, deep-research) and document processing (docs-bee) out of web-bee into academic-bee, closing the loop on the entire academic workflow. web-bee is now focused purely as a software engineering toolbox.

Final result: 5 Bees · 47 submodules · 0 standalone leftovers.

academic-bee   Full academic pipeline (search→read→write→review→store) web-bee        Software engineering toolbox study-bee      Code comprehension media-bee      Media content design-bee     Design output
