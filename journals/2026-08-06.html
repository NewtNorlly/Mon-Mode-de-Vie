今天起得比较早，大概是9点钟就起来了。

中午给妈妈打了电话，要到了我手机号的 PUK 码。之前表妹试错太多密码把 SIM 卡锁了，这一下总算有了着落。

下午我一直督促两个小老师认真学习，提提进度。我自己也顺便优化了网上的两个小细节。

晚上三舅和三舅妈回家了。三舅妈问我：「有没有搞好啦？」我很开心地说，搞好了，真方便啊！因为她之前也遇到过类似的问题，打个运营商的客服电话就直接搞定了。

三舅在晚上检查了两个小老师的学习进度。我的表妹的学习状态和学习成果，让父辈很满意。表弟呢，虽然也学了一些，也搞懂了一些，但是进度慢了一些，状态还不是特别兴奋。于是他老爹老妈就嘱咐他，周六周天就不要休息、不要玩了，一直学。你进度已经慢了一些啦，每天都要学，不要到最后慢了一大截，那就很吓人了。

晚上，总之就没怎么发火吧，大家都没怎么发火。诸事顺遂。

Got up fairly early today—around 9 o'clock.

Called Mom at noon and got the PUK code for my SIM card. After my girl cousin locked it with too many wrong password attempts, this was finally sorted.

In the afternoon I kept pushing the two young teachers to study hard and pick up the pace. I also tweaked a couple of small details online in the meantime.

In the evening, Third Uncle and Third Aunt came home. My aunt asked me, "Did you get it sorted?" I happily told her, all done—so convenient! She'd run into the same issue before, and a call to the carrier's customer service sorted it right out.

Third Uncle checked on the two young teachers' progress tonight. My girl cousin's learning attitude and results really satisfied the parents. My boy cousin, on the other hand, did learn some and understood some too, but his pace was slower, and he wasn't especially pumped about it. So his parents told him: no resting and no playing on Saturday or Sunday—just keep studying. You're already a bit behind. Study every day. Don't let it snowball into a huge gap by the end—that would be terrifying.

All in all, no one lost their temper tonight. A peaceful, smooth evening.
