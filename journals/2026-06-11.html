🏷️ 做题踩坑速记

  1. M=500/P 的理解：计算题3题干写"L=(0.5Y-500r)P，M=500/P"，实际含义是名义货币供给M=500，M/P=500/P为实际供给。L的那个P是名义货币需求写法（L名义=实际需求×P）。若理解反了会导致LM方程多一个P²。

🏷️ Quick Notes on Pitfalls While Solving

  1. Understanding M=500/P: Question 3's problem statement writes “L=(0.5Y-500r)P, M=500/P”; the actual meaning is nominal money supply M=500, with M/P=500/P as real supply. The P in L is the nominal money-demand formulation (nominal L = real demand × P). If understood backwards, the LM equation would gain an extra P².
  2. The relationship between the IS slope and d: the larger d is (the more sensitive investment is to the interest rate), the larger the absolute value of the IS slope, but the curve becomes flatter — because the same Δr triggers a larger ΔI, and hence a larger ΔY.
  3. Fiscal policy in the classical case: it does not affect total output, but it does affect the real interest rate and the composition of output. So one cannot say it has “no effect on real variables at all.”

🏷️ Notes rapides sur les pièges rencontrés

  1. Comprendre M=500/P : l'énoncé de la question 3 écrit « L=(0.5Y-500r)P, M=500/P » ; le sens réel est une offre de monnaie nominale M=500, avec M/P=500/P comme offre réelle.
